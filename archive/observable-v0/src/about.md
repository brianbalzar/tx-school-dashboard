# About the data

## Source

All financial figures come from the Texas Education Agency's **[Summarized PEIMS Actual Financial Data](https://tea.texas.gov/finance-and-grants/state-funding/state-funding-reports-and-data/peims-financial-data-downloads)**. That is one row per district per fiscal year, as districts reported them through PEIMS. The dashboard uses the **All Funds** columns, which include the general fund, special revenue, debt service and capital projects funds.

Fiscal years are labeled by the year the school year ends, so FY2025 means 2024-25. Enrollment is the PEIMS fall survey enrollment for that year.

County and education service center (ESC) region come from a TEA district directory extract.

## Metrics

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Plant maintenance & operations | Function 51: utilities, custodial, maintenance and grounds, per enrolled student |
| Capital projects | Total capital projects expenditures by object (6600), per student |
| Security & monitoring | Function 52, per student |
| Transportation | Function 34, per student |
| Data processing | Function 53, per student |
| Total disbursements | All expenditures and other uses, per student |
| Debt service | Total debt service expenditures by object (6500), per student |
| Plant M&O share | Function 51 ÷ total operating expenditures |
| Net margin | (Total revenue − total disbursements) ÷ total revenue |

**Peer percentile** ranks a district against every Texas district in the same enrollment band for the same year: under 500; 500–999; 1,000–1,599; 1,600–4,999; 5,000–24,999; 25,000+. A value of 90 means the district is higher than 90% of its peers.

**5-year change** compares the selected year with five years earlier. For percentage metrics, it is the change in percentage points.

## Caveats

- **Capital spending is lumpy.** It jumps in the years right after a bond passes, so one year's capital figure says little about a district's long-run spending.
- **Net margin is not an operating deficit.** Disbursements include spending of bond proceeds and other financing that is not counted as revenue. A negative margin often just means a district is spending down bond funds.
- **Charters are hidden by default.** Open-enrollment charters are identified by name, so any district whose name lacks ISD, CISD, MSD or CSD is treated as a charter. Many have small or fluctuating enrollment, which produces extreme per-student values.
- **The county map shows district medians.** Each county's color is the median of the districts headquartered there, not a total for the county.
