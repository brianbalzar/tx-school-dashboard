Methodology

What this tool can and cannot tell you.

The dashboard identifies credible, evidence-backed hypotheses. It cannot diagnose building condition or guarantee savings without district-specific facility and utility data.

Findings are phrased as observations, possible explanations and a question. All costs are per student; district square footage isn’t publicly available. “Utilities” means PEIMS utility spending, which combines electricity, gas, water, wastewater and telephone. The federal FY2020 figure is labeled “federal energy estimate (FY2020)”.

Operations peers match on {{ critOps }}. Capital peers match on {{ critCap }}. Comparisons use medians of the district, its peers, its ESC region and the state.

Full definitions, formulas, retrieval dates and known gaps are planned for the next pass.
